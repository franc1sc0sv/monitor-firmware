# Data hygiene

- `data/` (vendor firmware, dumps, Ghidra projects) and `out/` (images, logs) are ignored and must stay local.
- Do not paste dumps, decompiler output or settings dumps with serials into docs, commits, issues or chat summaries.
- Version maps hold hashes of stock bytes, never the bytes. Short descriptions of stock behaviour are fine.
- Screenshots of the UI settings grid can show the serial unless masked: keep masking on.
